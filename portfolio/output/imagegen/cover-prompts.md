# Portfolio cover regeneration

Mode: built-in image_gen. Preserve each original cover's brand and illustration style; improve square framing.

## Kali

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: redesigned square project cover for the portfolio.
Input image: the attached ORIGINAL kali cover is the visual source of truth for style, brand, palette, lettering and mascot.
Primary request: regenerate this cover while keeping its original kawaii sticker illustration style. This is a composition improvement, not a rebrand. Make the original's landscape-banner design feel intentionally composed for a full square cover.
Preserve the exact original visual language: warm cream background, deep forest-green and soft matcha-green rounded lowercase 'kali' bubble wordmark, white die-cut sticker outline, subtle offset gray sticker shadow, dark hand-drawn contours, cute little matcha cup mascot with foam, a tiny green sprout, rosy cheeks and friendly face. Small pink hearts, sage sparkles and a few cream receipt-paper motifs should use the same flat, hand-drawn sticker treatment.
Composition: 1:1 square. One large cohesive central sticker composition, occupying around 75 percent of square width and height, with comfortable cream margins on all sides. Put the large wordmark 'kali' in the upper middle, the smiling matcha cup prominently nestled under and beside it, and a sparse, balanced constellation of small supporting stickers around the composition. Use the vertical area meaningfully instead of putting a wide banner across an otherwise empty square. Keep the iconography sparse, charming, and visually ordered, readable at thumbnail size. Wordmark must be the dominant text.
Text (verbatim): 'kali'. No other text, no tagline, no tiny Japanese lettering, no gibberish.
Style constraints: keep the original's soft pastel kawaii illustrated sticker aesthetic, green and cream dominant with strawberry-pink accents, warm approachable feel, flat 2D with only the original's gentle sticker shadows. Do not turn this into 3D, glass, chrome, sci-fi art, a dark abstract render, a sleek corporate logo, or a photoreal object.
Output one finished square image, no presentation mockup, no borders, no collage, no watermark.
